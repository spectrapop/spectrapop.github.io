Herbarium Spectroscopy with MicroNIR

Theory and practice

Introduction

Welcome!😊

This course was designed to train students and professionals in the use of the portable MicroNIR spectrometer, covering everything from the initial setup of the equipment to the analysis of spectral data.

Over three days of theoretical and practical activities, participants will explore the fundamentals of near-infrared (NIR) spectroscopy and its application to plant identification.

In addition, the course seeks to identify practical challenges in the use of the equipment, contributing to the development of a standardized application protocol within the SpectraPop Project.

Course details

Where: PPG-Botany classroom and INPA Herbarium, National Institute for Amazonian Research (INPA, campus I), Av. André Araújo, 2.936 - 69067-375 - Petrópolis, Manaus, Amazonas, Brazil.

Date: December 17–19, 2025.

Target audience: Students and professionals involved in the herbarium/reference collection workflow who are interested in using portable spectroscopy for scientific research.

Number of places: 20 (in-person places).

Course load: 18 h (a certificate of participation will be issued).

Format: Hybrid, with theoretical classes given in a virtual environment (with the option of attending in person in a classroom at INPA) and practical classes exclusively in person. Four laptops will be available for the practical activities, and participants will be organized into four groups.

Material: Slides, scripts and data obtained during the course will be made available directly to the students or via GitHub.

Important notes:

  • External participants must present a photo ID (e.g., national ID card) and inform staff at any INPA gate that they are taking part in a training at the INPA Herbarium.

  • At the end of the course, each participant must fill in an evaluation form to assess the training and to give suggestions for improving the MicroNIR usage protocol, which will later be made available to the entire interested community.
